# Roadmap

The roadmap expresses direction, not a delivery promise.

## Beta quality

- Physical testing across Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola, and other
  common Android devices, including dual-SIM configurations
- Median, p95, and maximum screening-decision latency measurements
- Accessibility audit with TalkBack, large text, contrast, and switch access
- Two independent native-speaker reviews for every locale
- Reproducible release-signing and maintainer recovery documentation

## Product value

- Local allow/silence rules beyond contacts
- Explainable, deterministic on-device risk signals permitted by Android APIs
- Post-call feedback without collecting or uploading call data
- Import/export of user rules with explicit consent and encryption

## Ecosystem

- OEM or carrier research for legitimate privileged on-device voice screening
- Clear separation between universally available Android features and any
  partner-only functionality

## Explicitly out of scope today

- Claiming live AI cellular-call conversations in the public Android app
- Recording calls or bypassing platform security restrictions
- Uploading contacts, phone numbers, or call metadata
- iOS parity where public APIs do not permit equivalent behavior
