# Community code of conduct

Pehredar is committed to a welcoming, safe, and respectful community.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different languages, cultures, identities, and levels of experience.
- Protect privacy. Never publish another person's phone number, contact data,
  call details, recordings, or private correspondence.
- Accept maintainers' decisions gracefully and help improve the process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate
misinformation, spam, or sustained disruption are not tolerated. Attempts to
obtain or expose sensitive call or contact information are especially serious.

## Enforcement

Report community incidents privately through the repository owner's GitHub
profile contact channel. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive
details. Maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily
restrict, or permanently ban participants. Reports are reviewed confidentially
and without retaliation. Decisions consider severity, context, and community
safety.

This policy applies to project spaces and to public conduct representing the
project. Maintainers must recuse themselves from reports in which they have a
conflict of interest.
